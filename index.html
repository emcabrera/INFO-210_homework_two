Moves

  • Sweet Veil: Prevents itself and allies from falling asleep
  • Decorate: Boosts an ally's attack & special attack; unique to Alcremie
  • Play Rough: A strong fairy-type physical attack
  • Misty Explosion: Deals damage to adjacent Pokémon, useful in doubles
  • Recover: Restores Alcremie's HP, enhancing its longevity in battle

Alcremie Information

Alcremie is commonly found in bakeries due to it being the main provider of cream for their sweets, the happier she is, the richer the cream. She can also be found in beauty pageants since she has customizable cream decorations. Her name actually comes from "alchemy" and "cream", due to her magical and dessert like qualities. She has about 63 different forms, all in different flavors and decorations, along with a unique Gigantamax form, turning into a giant towering cake form.
